PALMA BAY

A top-down open-world crime game. Original story, original city, no Rockstar assets.

You are Marco, a driver trying to pay off a debt in Palma Bay. Steal a car, run jobs for Dana, and stay one step ahead of the Palma Bay Police.

Controls
WASD — move / drive  ·  Shift — sprint
E — enter / exit a car  ·  Mouse click or Space — shoot
P or Esc — pause  ·  R — restart after Wasted / Busted

Single file, offline. Progress saves locally in your browser.
